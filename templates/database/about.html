ABOUT COLLEGEHUB

College information with provenance.

CollegeHub is being developed to help students explore Tamil Nadu higher-education institutions and admission information.

Data integrity

Public database-mode pages read the universal SQLAlchemy database. CollegeHub does not substitute sample records when production data is missing. Each factual record should show its source, academic year and verification status. Confirm changing admissions information with the responsible official authority.

Official records and AI features are only available when collected, reviewed and tested. An empty result means information is unavailable; it does not mean zero.
