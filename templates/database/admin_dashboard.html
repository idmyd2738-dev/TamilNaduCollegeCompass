Available official imports

The Anna University directory importer is bounded to its configured official source. New records are pending review.

TNGASA 2026 government arts and science college directory

Imports college codes, names, and districts from the official 2026 college-information PDF. NAAC grades are stored as pending accreditation records. The source's 2025 admit percentages, NIRF labels and seat-availability fields are retained in provenance notes; they are not converted into cutoff or programme seat records.

Index an official document for the AI assistant

Register the official source first. Upload a PDF, HTML, CSV, TXT or Markdown document. Text is extracted, chunked and embedded; the original file is not retained.

AI indexing requires the configured embedding provider. Chat responses also require a configured LLM provider.
