ABOUT THE DEMO

One starting point.

This prototype demonstrates how college discovery information could be organized in one accessible place.

THE IDEA

Make exploration easier.

Students and families often need to visit multiple sources to understand institutions, programmes and campus facilities. College Compass is a concept for bringing discovery tools together.

This demonstration includes fictional sample institutions and predefined assistant responses. It does not provide official admissions information.
